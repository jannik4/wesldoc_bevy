Source

3x3 bilaterial filter (edge-preserving blur) https://people.csail.mit.edu/sparis/bf_course/course_notes.pdf

Note: Does not use the Gaussian kernel part of a typical bilateral blur From the paper: "use the information gathered on a neighborhood of 4 × 4 using a bilateral filter for reconstruction, using uniform convolution weights"

Note: The paper does a 4x4 (not quite centered) filter, offset by +/- 1 pixel every other frame XeGTAO does a 3x3 filter, on two pixels at a time per compute thread, applied twice We do a 3x3 filter, on 1 pixel per compute thread, applied once
