bevy_pbr::ssr::raymarch::DepthRayMarchResult

Source 
struct DepthRayMarchResult {
    hit: bool,
    hit_t: f32,
    hit_uv: vec2<f32>,
    hit_penetration: f32,
    hit_penetration_frac: f32
}

Fields

hit: bool

True if the raymarch hit something.

hit_t: f32

In case of a hit, the normalized distance to it.

In case of a miss, the furthest the ray managed to travel, which could either be exceeding the max range, or getting behind a surface further than the depth thickness.

Range: 0..=1 as a lerp factor over ray_start_cs..=ray_end_cs.

hit_uv: vec2<f32>

UV corresponding to hit_t.

hit_penetration: f32

The distance that the hit point penetrates into the hit surface. Will normally be non-zero due to limited precision of the ray march.

In case of a miss: undefined.

hit_penetration_frac: f32

Ditto, within the range 0..DepthRayMarch::depth_thickness_linear_z

In case of a miss: undefined.
