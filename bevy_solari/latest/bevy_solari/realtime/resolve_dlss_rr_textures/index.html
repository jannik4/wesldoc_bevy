Constants

  • ENVIRONMENT_VIRTUAL_DISTANCE

    Distance, in meters, standing in for "infinitely far" when a mirror ray finds nothing.

  • FULL_REPLACEMENT_FRACTION

    A surface is replaced outright only when nearly all of its reflectance leaves through the delta lobe.

  • MAX_VIRTUAL_DEPTH_JITTER

    How far a reflector's virtual image may shift between neighboring pixels, as a fraction of the virtual distance itself, before the surface is treated as too curved to replace.

  • NORMAL_QUANTIZATION_FLOOR

    Even a perfectly flat surface measures a small nonzero normal change, because G-buffer normals are 24-bit octahedral and neighboring texels round differently.
